# SEO Using AI: brand kit for profiles and listings

Google and AI tools such as ChatGPT learn who a brand is by reading many websites and checking that they agree. Use the exact wording below everywhere so every source tells the same story. When you create a profile, add its URL to `social` in `src/lib/site.ts`. That feeds the Organization schema on the website, which links the profiles to the domain.

## Where to create profiles, in priority order

1. **Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.** Not profiles, but required. Bing feeds ChatGPT and Copilot.
2. **LinkedIn company page.** Most trusted business profile for both Google and AI tools.
3. **YouTube channel.** Short screen recordings of each guide. Video results rank on Google and are cited by AI.
4. **X and Facebook pages.** Claim the handle and post each new guide.
5. **Crunchbase and Clutch.** Business directories that AI tools quote for "best agency" questions.
6. **Reddit and relevant communities.** Take part as a real person. Never post fake recommendations.

## Rules

- Same name, same description, same logo, same link on every profile.
- Link every profile back to https://seousingai.com.
- Never buy reviews, followers, or links. It breaks platform rules and the damage outlasts the benefit.
