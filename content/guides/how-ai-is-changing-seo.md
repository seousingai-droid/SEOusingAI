## How is AI changing SEO?

AI is changing SEO in two directions. It is changing how people get answers, and it is changing how SEO work gets done. Both are happening at the same time, which is why advice from even two years ago can feel out of date.

The short version: ranking first is no longer the finish line, and doing SEO by hand is no longer necessary.

![Two-sided diagram of how AI is changing SEO: on the search side, AI answers and fewer clicks; on the work side, faster audits, research, drafts, and reports](/images/how-ai-is-changing-seo.webp "AI changed both where answers appear and how SEO work gets done.")

## What is the effect of AI on search clicks?

The effect of AI on search clicks is a measurable drop on the searches where an AI answer appears. Three public studies show the scale.

| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| Users clicked a traditional result in 8% of visits with an AI summary, versus 15% without | [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) |
| About 18% of Google searches in the study produced an AI summary, and users clicked a link inside the summary in 1% of visits | Pew Research Center, same study |
| AI Overviews appeared on 6.49% of tracked queries in January 2025, peaked at 24.61% in July, and settled at 15.69% in November | [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/semrush-ai-overviews-study/) |
| Less than one third of Google searches still send a click to the open web | [SparkToro, 2026](https://sparktoro.com/blog/in-2026-less-than-one-third-of-google-searches-still-send-a-click/) |

Two details matter here. Pew found AI summaries far more often on long, question-style searches than on one or two word searches. And Semrush's tracking shows they appear mostly on informational searches, the kind that start with how, what, or why. The informational click is shrinking. Searches where someone is ready to buy or book are less affected so far.

## What does Google say about ranking in its AI features?

Google says there is nothing extra to do. Its [documentation on AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) states that there are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, and that site owners do not need to create new machine-readable files, AI text files, or special markup.

In plain terms, Google's AI features draw on the same index and the same quality signals as regular search. A page that is crawlable, helpful, and trusted is eligible. That is why this site treats AI search optimization as an extension of good SEO, not a replacement for it.

## How has AI changed the way SEO work gets done?

AI has changed SEO work by removing most of the manual hours. Tasks that took days now take minutes.

- **Audits.** AI can read every page of a site, not a sample.
- **Research.** Hundreds of keywords grouped by what the searcher wants, in one pass.
- **Drafting.** Outlines, first drafts, titles, and descriptions on demand.
- **Code.** Schema, redirects, and fixes written and explained.
- **Reporting.** Exports turned into plain-English summaries.

What AI has not changed is the need for a person to check facts, add real experience, and decide what matters. See [can AI do SEO](/guides/can-ai-do-seo) for the full split.

## What still works in SEO in 2026?

What still works in SEO in 2026 is what Google has asked for all along, done more carefully.

1. **Helpful, first-hand content.** Google's [helpful content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) asks whether content shows real experience and leaves the reader satisfied.
2. **Searches with buying intent.** Service, product, comparison, and local searches still send clicks.
3. **A brand people search for.** Someone who types your name is not diverted by an AI summary.
4. **Mentions on other websites.** Reviews, directories, press, and communities shape both rankings and what AI tools say about you.
5. **Technical health.** Fast, crawlable, mobile-friendly pages with clear structure.

## What should you do differently now?

Do three things differently. First, stop measuring success by traffic alone. Track calls, bookings, and sales from search. Second, write every section so it can stand alone as an answer, because that is what AI tools quote. Third, put effort into being mentioned off your own website.
