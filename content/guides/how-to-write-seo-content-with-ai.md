## How do you write SEO content with AI?

You write SEO content with AI in five steps: brief, draft, verify, enrich, and optimize. AI leads the first two. A person leads the next two. You share the last.

1. **Brief.** Paste the titles and headings of the top results into the AI and ask what they cover, what they miss, and what format they use.
2. **Draft.** Have the AI write one section at a time from the brief, starting each section with a direct answer.
3. **Verify.** Check every number, name, price, and claim against an original source. Delete what you cannot confirm.
4. **Enrich.** Add what only you know: your process, your prices, real examples, photos, and opinions.
5. **Optimize.** Write the title and description, add internal links, and add FAQ and schema.

## Why start with a content brief?

Start with a content brief because AI without direction produces the average of everything it has read. A brief gives it your angle.

A useful brief fits on one page: the search query, what the searcher wants, the headings phrased as questions, the facts to include with their sources, and one sentence on what this page adds that others lack. ## How do you get a good first draft from an AI writer?

You get a good first draft from an AI writer by giving it material and limits. Give it the brief, your notes, and any data you have. Then set rules.

```
Write the section under this heading: "[heading]".
First sentence must answer the heading directly.
Keep it under 170 words and make it understandable on its own.
Use only the facts in my notes. Mark anything uncertain with [verify].
Plain American English. No filler, no hype.
```

Drafting section by section keeps quality higher than asking for a whole article at once, and it makes each section easy to check.

## What must a person add?

A person must add the two things AI cannot: verified facts and first-hand experience. These are also the two things Google's quality guidelines emphasize most, under the label E-E-A-T, which stands for experience, expertise, authoritativeness, and trust.

In practice, replace general statements with specifics. "Prices vary" becomes what you actually charge and why. "Many customers ask" becomes the real question a customer asked last week. One honest paragraph of experience does more for a page than five paragraphs of polished filler.

## How do you make AI content quotable by AI answers?

You make content quotable by AI answers by writing each section so it can stand alone. AI tools lift short passages, not whole pages.

- Phrase headings as the questions people ask.
- Answer in the first sentence under each heading.
- Name the subject in each section so it needs no surrounding context.
- Attach a named source to every statistic.
- Show a last updated date.

## What mistakes ruin AI content?

The mistakes that ruin AI content are publishing without checking facts, accepting generic phrasing, targeting the same keyword with several pages, and padding for length. Each one makes a page less useful, and usefulness is what both Google and AI tools reward.
