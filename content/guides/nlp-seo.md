## What is NLP SEO?

NLP SEO is the practice of making content easy for language-understanding search engines to interpret. NLP stands for natural language processing, the part of AI that lets computers work out what human language means.

For a business owner, the practical point is simple. Google reads for meaning. It rewards pages that explain things clearly, and it is no longer fooled by pages that repeat a phrase twenty times.

## How does Google use NLP?

Google uses NLP to understand both the search and the page. Three public milestones show how this developed.

- **BERT, 2019.** Google [announced](https://blog.google/products/search/search-language-understanding-bert/) that this language model would help it better understand one in 10 searches in the U.S. in English, especially longer, conversational ones where small words such as "for" and "to" change the meaning.
- **MUM, 2021.** Google [introduced](https://blog.google/products/search/introducing-mum/) a more powerful model designed to understand information across languages and formats.
- **AI Overviews and AI Mode.** Google's AI answers build on the same ability to read a passage and judge whether it answers the question.

The direction is consistent. Each step made Google better at matching meaning, and less dependent on exact keyword matches.

## What are entities, and why do they matter?

Entities are specific things a search engine can identify: a business, a person, a city, a product, a concept. Google connects entities to each other in a large database and uses those connections to understand content.

When your page clearly states "Harbor Dental is a family dentist in Portland, Maine, offering cleanings, fillings, and Invisalign", it names five or six entities and how they relate. That single sentence tells a search engine more than a paragraph of vague marketing language.

## How do you write for NLP?

You write for NLP by writing clearly for people. These habits help most.

1. **Answer first.** Put the direct answer in the first sentence under each heading.
2. **Use plain sentence structure.** Subject, verb, object. Short sentences are easier for people and machines.
3. **Name things.** Use the actual names of your business, location, services, and products. Avoid "we" and "it" where a name would be clearer.
4. **Cover the topic the way an expert would.** Include the related questions a knowledgeable person would expect.
5. **Use real headings, lists, and tables.** Structure shows how ideas relate.
6. **Add schema.** Structured data states your key facts in a format with no ambiguity. See [technical SEO](/services/technical-seo).

## What should you stop doing?

Stop keyword stuffing, stop writing long introductions before the answer, and stop creating separate pages for tiny keyword variations such as "plumber Portland" and "Portland plumber". NLP lets Google see that these mean the same thing, so the extra pages only compete with each other.

## How does NLP connect to AI answers?

NLP connects to AI answers because tools like ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews are built on the same kind of language understanding. They select passages that clearly and completely answer a question. Content written for NLP is, almost by definition, content that AI tools can quote.
